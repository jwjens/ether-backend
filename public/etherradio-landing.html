Professional broadcast tools, finally designed for humans.

3-Deck Live Assist
Primary, Standby, and Next Up decks with real-time waveforms, VU meters, and auto-crossfade. Rust audio engine, sub-10ms latency.
Widget Canvas
Build your own layout. Drag any panel anywhere, resize like a window. Save named layouts per show. Adobe-style — no developer mode.
Album Art Reveal
During the intro countdown, full-bleed album art fills the deck. 600×600 from iTunes. Pulses with the music. Jocks feel the song.
Auto-Duck
Music drops to 18% the moment your mic goes live. Smoothly restores when you cut. One toggle. Every broadcast sounds mixed.
Format Clocks
Visual hour clock designer with drag-and-drop segments. Song categories, breaks, talk sets. Live preview updates as you build.
AI Show Notes
One click writes your show notes after every session. Reads the play log, generates description and music credits. Paste into RSS.
